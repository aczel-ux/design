The Schönherz Brass Band began in an engineering campus community and grew into a regular performing group with a singer and an expanding audience.

They wanted an identity that felt fun and approachable while remaining simple and clearly communicating the instrument at the heart of the band. The resulting system includes flexible logo arrangements suitable for different formats and performance materials.
