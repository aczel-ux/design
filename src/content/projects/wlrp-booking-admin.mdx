WLRP operates a range of tourism and entertainment services in Budapest. Its administration system gives organizers, managers, partners and on-site colleagues role-specific tools for booking management and customer relationship workflows.

Online bookings arrive directly in the system, while staff can also record and edit bookings manually. Clear states communicate payment and operational status.

## A flexible interface

The services share some properties, but no two have identical requirements. The interface was built from modules that could be assembled around new service parameters without redesigning the whole application.

## Designed for the office and the field

The system is used on desktop computers and touch-based tablets. Navigation needed to be fast for staff working with customers on location. Filters and permissions adapt the same system to different roles and partner venues.

The information structure also retained enough familiarity from the organization’s previous spreadsheet workflow to reduce training time for new colleagues.
