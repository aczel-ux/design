GoToMeeting is a video-conferencing product used by organizations ranging from small businesses to large enterprises.

The product family included native Windows and Mac applications, iOS and Android apps and a browser experience. Each endpoint was adapted to the conventions and capabilities of its platform while remaining part of one product.

GoToMeeting was also available on Facebook Portal from its launch. Working with Facebook, we adapted the Android application to the capabilities and interaction patterns of the Portal devices.
