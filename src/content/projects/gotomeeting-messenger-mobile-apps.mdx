Messenger was a lightweight chat application that complemented the main GoToMeeting product. Individuals and groups could continue conversations outside meetings and start a call directly from a chat.

The goal was not to compete feature-for-feature with Slack or Microsoft Teams. Instead, the product provided a simpler, focused layer of value around the meeting experience.

I was the first designer dedicated to the product, working across its browser, iOS and Android experiences from the beginning of the project.

## Strategy and technology

Messenger was not sold separately. It helped GoToMeeting become more useful before and after a scheduled call and allowed the team to explore a broader collaboration experience.

The mobile applications were native, so patterns could be adapted to each platform while preserving a coherent product identity.
