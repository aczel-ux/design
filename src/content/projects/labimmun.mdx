Labimmun is a medical diagnostics provider specializing in tools for autoimmune disease diagnostics.

The company wanted to refresh its identity without discarding the recognition attached to the existing symbol. We retained that core idea and paired it with a modern, more humanistic typeface, producing a clearer and more flexible family of logo configurations.
