In 2021, LogMeIn became GoTo and its products began moving into one centralized application. GoToMeeting, GoToWebinar, GoToConnect, GoToTraining and Messenger were brought together as modules of the same experience across desktop, browser, mobile and tablet.

## A wider product context

The organizational shift brought previously isolated product teams together across continents and time zones. We used shared collaborative spaces to keep decisions visible and allow work to continue asynchronously.

The Meetings module was designed to replace several mature products while preserving the capability that existing customers relied on. Tablet support, which had often been secondary, became a first-class part of the design.

## My role

My role evolved from GoToMeeting designer to Meeting Experience Designer. I helped define the UX vision and tactical roadmap for meetings, aligned the work with other product modules, and ensured the mobile experience followed the shared design system and accessibility standards.
