Most customers of WLRP’s Budapest activities are visitors who may use an individual service only once. Traditional long-term retention was therefore less useful than helping people discover complementary experiences during the short period when they were still in the city.

## Why a progressive web app?

A progressive web app offered an app-like, responsive experience without requiring visitors to find and install a native application. The technology allowed the product to load quickly, respond smoothly and be added to supported home screens while remaining accessible from an ordinary link.

The experience created a lightweight channel for introducing relevant services at the moment they were most useful.
