OrthoGraph’s tablet interface was built from a coherent style and symbol system. Familiar visual conventions and a color palette with clear internal logic helped a broad professional audience follow the most effective workflow.

This project was the predecessor to OrthoGraph I and established many of the interaction and visual principles that informed the later cross-platform product.
