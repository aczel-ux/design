Miratia is an interactive city-puzzle app created for families traveling with children. The website redesign gives visitors a clear teaser of the application’s fresh, playful experience.

## Website concept

The site inherits the app’s bold colors, simple icons and typography, paired with illustrations by László Kiss. Atmospheric photography grounds the playful interface in the real cities where the adventures take place.

## Mobile first

Because the product is discovered and used on mobile devices, the responsive experience was a primary requirement. Content hierarchy, calls to action and visual pacing were designed to remain clear on smaller screens rather than being reduced from a desktop-only composition.
